Bank logos in this folder are exported from @iran-utils/iranian-banks-icons-core v1.0.4
(https://github.com/houtan-rocky/iranian-banks-react-icons), optimized with svgo.
Logos are trademarks of their respective banks and are used only to identify them.
ewano.svg (Ewano card) wraps the Ewano logo (a raster image, scaled to 128 px) supplied by the project owner.
